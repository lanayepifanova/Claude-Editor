Why has networking become the bottleneck in AI? Aravindh Srikumar of Upscale AI talks with Steven Hou, Head of Research at Silicon Data, about why compute has scaled 25X in six years while network bandwidth grew only 8X, and what it takes to make a distributed cluster behave like a single computer.

Silicon Data is the independent referee of the compute market. It publishes GPU price indices and benchmarks and holds no positions of its own, so it can measure supply and demand without bias. Its indices are the basis for compute futures with CME Group.

Upscale AI builds networking for AI clusters only, designed to work with any GPU or accelerator. It announced its Token Fabric architecture at the NeoCloud Summit and works with Nvidia on scale-out systems.

In this conversation:
• Why compute grew 25X while networks grew 8X, and why that leaves GPUs waiting
• Token Fabric: running scale-up and scale-out as one network
• Why the stack is built on open standards such as Ethernet and SONiC
• How Ethernet-based scale-up sits alongside Nvidia's NVLink
• What a networking company focused only on AI does differently
