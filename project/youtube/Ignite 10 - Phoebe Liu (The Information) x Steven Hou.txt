YouTube title: Why Token Prices Are Falling, and What It Means for AI Valuations

YouTube description:

Why are AI token prices falling, and what does that mean for frontier labs? Steven Hou, Head of Research at Silicon Data, talks with Phoebe Liu of The Information about how GPU price indices are built from quoted and transaction prices, and why cheap everyday models pull the average token price down.

Silicon Data is the independent referee of the compute market. It publishes GPU price indices and benchmarks and holds no positions of its own, so it can measure supply and demand without bias. Its indices are the basis for compute futures with CME Group.

In this conversation:
• How Silicon Data's GPU indices combine quoted and transaction prices
• Adjusting for location, specs, term length and provider type
• How providers qualify for the index, and how they share their data
• What the token index really measures, and what it says about frontier lab valuations
• Rising GPU rental rates, and why power, not chips, is now the shortage
