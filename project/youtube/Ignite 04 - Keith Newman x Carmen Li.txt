YouTube title: Is AI Compute a Bubble? The CEO Building GPU Futures Explains

YouTube description:

Is AI compute in a bubble? Carmen Li, CEO of Silicon Data and Compute Exchange, and Keith Newman explain how GPUs are turning into a tradable commodity, and why markets need futures to find the real price of compute.

Silicon Data is the independent referee of the compute market. It publishes GPU price indices and benchmarks and holds no positions of its own, so it can measure supply and demand without bias. Its indices are the basis for compute futures with CME Group.

Compute Exchange is a neutral marketplace for buying and selling physical compute. It works like a NASDAQ for GPUs, connecting buyers with more than 150 providers worldwide. It now handles over $1 billion in quote requests every month, plus secondhand GPU sales.

In this conversation:
• Why the compute market needs more transparency and risk management
• Why GPU pricing is maturing but token pricing is still hard to pin down
• Whether we’re in an AI bubble, and why futures help with price discovery
• What’s next: forward contracts leading to physically delivered futures
