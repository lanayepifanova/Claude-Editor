YouTube title: Why AI Data Centers Wait 7 Years to Plug Into the Grid

YouTube description:

Why can it take seven years to connect a data center to the grid? Bianca of Ambra Compute, a former Citadel quant with a background in power trading, talks with Steven Hou, Head of Research at Silicon Data, about why power is the bottleneck for AI, and why small, distributed data centers can come online in months instead.

Silicon Data is the independent referee of the compute market. It publishes GPU price indices and benchmarks and holds no positions of its own, so it can measure supply and demand without bias. Its indices are the basis for compute futures with CME Group.

Ambra Compute is a startup that builds small, containerized data centers where there is spare power capacity, so compute can come online in about 90 days.

In this conversation:
• Why the grid connection, not the building, is the main constraint, and how going small cuts permitting from years to weeks
• Why inference suits smaller, distributed clusters running on cheap power
• Running on several types of chips rather than one
• Congestion, curtailment, and why solar power sometimes sells at negative prices
• The build-out: roughly 100 megawatts by the end of 2027, mostly in Europe
