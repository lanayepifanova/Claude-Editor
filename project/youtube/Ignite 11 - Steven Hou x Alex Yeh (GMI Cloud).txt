YouTube title: How Bitcoin Miners Became AI Clouds: A Founder Who Made the Switch

YouTube description:

Why are so many Bitcoin miners turning into AI clouds? Alex Yeh, founder of GMI Cloud, talks with Steven Hou, Head of Research at Silicon Data, about switching from Bitcoin mining to GPU cloud, and why running a cloud comes down to filling every gap in utilization.

Silicon Data is the independent referee of the compute market. It publishes GPU price indices and benchmarks and holds no positions of its own, so it can measure supply and demand without bias. Its indices are the basis for compute futures with CME Group.

GMI Cloud spent its first two years in Bitcoin mining before moving into GPU cloud. It is built as a vertical stack, from bare metal and Kubernetes up to inference and agents, with clusters in the US and Asia.

In this conversation:
• The switch from Bitcoin mining to AI cloud, and what carried over: land, power and data center experience
• The cloud business model as a glass filled with ice, pebbles, sand and water
• Why operations, not code, is the hard part of running GPU clusters
• Why an AI infrastructure delivery date is a promise
• Running data centers across the US and Asia amid political headwinds
