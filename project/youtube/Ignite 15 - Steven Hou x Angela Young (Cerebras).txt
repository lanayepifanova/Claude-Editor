YouTube title: Why Build a Chip 50 Times Bigger Than a GPU?

YouTube description:

Why would anyone build a chip 50 times larger than a GPU? Angela of Cerebras talks with Steven Hou, Head of Research at Silicon Data, about what makes an inference chip different, why speed matters for interactive AI, and whether the wave of new inference chips will consolidate.

Silicon Data is the independent referee of the compute market. It publishes GPU price indices and benchmarks and holds no positions of its own, so it can measure supply and demand without bias. Its indices are the basis for compute futures with CME Group.

Cerebras designs and manufactures its own wafer-scale chips, used for both training and inference. It sells tokens through its own cloud and also sells hardware to neoclouds.

In this conversation:
• The wafer-scale chip, and the power and cooling it takes to run it
• Training versus inference, and why interactive workloads need speed
• Why so many new inference chips are appearing, and whether they will consolidate
• Why Cerebras runs its own cloud and also sells hardware to neoclouds
• Power as the industry's currency, and stacking memory on top of compute
