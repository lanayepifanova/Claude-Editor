1. **Glass of ice** — opens "What is a cloud, right? What metrics are you judged on? It's on the
   uptime… utilization rate." Then the analogy: big blocks of ice = bare-metal 5–6 year
   contracts, pebbles = short-term contracts, sand = token business / dedicated endpoints, water
   to fill the rest. Lands on "So it's all about filling the cracks… that is the fundamental core
   business model of a cloud." Vivid, visual, self-contained. Strongest clip.
2. **Pushing people** — "I think fundamentally it's very difficult for a software tech company
   going into operations… 24/7, SLA 99.95, what does that mean? That means swapping the parts
   when things are not going well." Through GPUs, networking, power, cooling, procurement. Ends
   "Software companies are like, oh, I push a code, it's done. This is pushing people."
   *Optional cold open:* Steven at 5:54.56 "How do you differentiate from someone like Together AI
   that's also getting into providing compute?" (≈5s; would push it over 60s).
3. **Bitcoin → AI** — "I bought a ton of land and power in rural America, which is kind of similar
   to what we're doing right now." Customers told him about GPT-3, "Alex, spare some cards…
   we'll pay," "it took me 10 months for this transition… it's nothing but smooth." Ends on the
   list "software team, infrastructure, financing, marketing, everything."
4. **Delivery is a promise** — Steven: "There are now many, many neoclouds, it's getting a very
   crowded space. How do you see this market shaking out?" Alex: "Just give me a takeaway… forget
   everything that I say… number one is reliability and on time. Customer delivery is a promise."
   Ends "So number one: supply chain." His filler "Yeah, really great question" is cut.
5. **Containers** — "I think we are actually the first wave of NCPs that got in… I was the one that
   innovated with modular data center. Back then I just called it containers. Now it's modular,
   sounds more fancy." Then why crypto founders moved faster than VCs: "the right foundation and
   the right team, a lot of ex-HPC folks that jumped into Bitcoin with me."
6. **Asia** — "As we face a lot of political headwinds in the U.S., we have pockets of power and
   data-center capacity all over the world, Asia and U.S." Helping U.S. companies land a
   beachhead in Asia and vice versa; "actual W2 employees in Indonesia and Malaysia and Singapore
   and Thailand and Taiwan and Japan and South Korea… people actually on the ground."
   Leading "Number two is" trimmed off.
