# Silicon Data brand spec for video

Source: "261026_Silicon Data Brand Guidelines_v.01" (Google Slides, Oct 2026), in
the Silicon Data Brand drive under General > Documentation. Fonts and logos are
in sibling Drive folders. Applies to all Silicon Data / Ignite videos and
replaces the gold + Montserrat styling used in R1.

## Color (deck p.37, p.41, p.42)
| Name | Hex | Use on video |
|---|---|---|
| Sky | `#67DEF9` | The highlight colour: spoken-word highlights |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Caption text, name on cards |
| Rich Black | `#09090B` | Caption outline, ground |
| Surface | `#18181B` (border `#27272A`) | Dark cards / panels |
| Slate | `#9E9FA9` | Muted secondary text on dark |
| Index Blue | `#2151D1` | Do NOT use on dark. p.22: blue on black is too dark (3.0:1) |

Sky is "our blue" on video: footage is a dark ground, and the deck reserves Index
Blue for white grounds. Sky never goes on a light ground.

## Type (deck p.28, p.30)
- **Inter** carries captions, labels and roles. Deck: Regular 400 for captions,
  Medium 500 where text has to be found quickly.
- **Instrument Sans** 400 is the voice: names and headlines, set light, never heavy.
- Both are open licence on Google Fonts.

## Logo (deck p.18-22)
- White lockup, top left, from the master artwork only, at its own proportions.
- No stretching, no shadows, glows or effects, always one flat colour.
- Deck's proposed scaling (portrait 1/4 width, landscape 1/8 width, inset by logo
  height) is marked "proposed". Existing approved sizes and positions stay until
  someone asks to change them.
