1. **Minus $10** — opens "If you take congestion to the extreme, you get to curtailment."
   Sun shining, wind blowing, oversupply: "at 12pm in California, if you own a solar farm…
   you're actually getting minus 10." Lands on "there's a lot of energy that is wasted and
   we're using that energy." Concrete number, clean thesis payoff. Strongest clip.
2. **Seven years** — "The main constraint is not building a data center, the main constraint
   is connecting the data center to the electrical grid." DSO/ISO permitting up to seven
   years vs 45–90 days below the threshold. Ends "…in 45 days rather than seven years."
3. **Energy-first neocloud** — "Ambra Compute is a startup, we just came out of stealth…
   multi-silicon token factory… I was a quant at Citadel… power being the bottleneck."
   Containerised micro data centres up in 90 days. Ends "a sort of energy-first neocloud,
   if you want to think about it." Company-intro short.
4. **Training vs inference** — "a big shift… from training-centric to inference-centric…
   hyperscaler clusters have been designed for training rather than inference. Inference runs
   much better in small distributed infrastructures… on very cheap power." Ends "they need
   compute to serve inference."
5. **Time value of compute** — Steven: "a very interesting analogy" (heterogeneous inference
   chips ↔ small clusters, fast to deploy). Bianca: "you're an economist, so — time value of
   compute… nobody wants to commit for a year from now… 45 days makes a massive difference for
   your cash flow cycle."
6. **Infinite demand** — "As you know, the demand for AI is just infinite… the scarce piece is
   energy… by end of 2027 roughly 100 megawatts… primarily in Europe… North America… massive
   demand in Asia and the Middle East as well." Number close.

**Removed 2026-10-27 as ads:** #3 is the company intro ("Ambra Compute is a startup…"); #6 turns
into "we've done an amazing job locking sites… 100 MW by 2027".

7. **Congestion** (replacement) — Steven: "Right now, where are you finding these pockets, so to
   speak?" Bianca: "I'm a trader by background… what you trade is congestion, and congestion
   arises when there's a difference in price between supply and demand… because the energy
   cannot flow because there's not enough capacity." Teaches a grid-market mechanism; no company
   claim in it (the margins brag just before, at 7:34–7:45, is excluded). Ends at 8:18.3, just before
   #1's in-point, so it plays as a prequel to #1 without overlapping. 28.3s after the silence
   pass, under the 30s floor: it can't be widened, because the head is the margins pitch and the tail is #1.
