3. **Energy-first neocloud** — "Ambra Compute is a startup, we just came out of stealth…

   if you want to think about it." Company-intro short.
4. **Training vs inference** — "a big shift… from training-centric to inference-centric…
   hyperscaler clusters have been designed for training rather than inference. Inference runs
   much better in small distributed infrastructures… on very cheap power." Ends "they need
   compute to serve inference."
5. **Time value of compute** — Steven: "a very interesting analogy" (heterogeneous inference
   chips ↔ small clusters, fast to deploy). Bianca: "you're an economist, so — time value of
   compute… nobody wants to commit for a year from now… 45 days makes a massive difference for
   your cash flow cycle."
6. **Infinite demand** — "As you know, the demand for AI is just infinite… the scarce piece is
   energy… by end of 2027 roughly 100 megawatts… primarily in Europe… North America… massive
   demand in Asia and the Middle East as well." Number close.
