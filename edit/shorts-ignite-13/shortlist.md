1. **Cache hit** — opens "The better the cache efficiency, the more tokens you deliver." Walks
   the 90% vs 98% cache-hit example: at 90% your GPUs work on 10% of queries, at 98% only 2%,
   "a five times difference." Lands on "we just increased the token amount that the same token
   factory delivers by five times." Number hook and number payoff. Strongest clip.
2. **Inference is economics** — "Inference is a fundamentally different problem. Inference is
   how everyone is actually making money out of AI." Power, capital, OPEX, value of tokens.
   Ends "People that do it well solve it in a fundamentally different way."
3. **Only a few labs win** — "It's about what's the biggest, craziest thing you can build to help
   you win… almost no question of economics or even reason." Dozens-to-hundreds of labs, only
   one, two or three win, so "spending is not a problem… if you don't make it to the end, it all
   didn't matter." Natural pair with #2 (training vs inference) but a separate point.
   *Alt out:* run on to 5:08.5 to include the Formula One line (≈64s, too long as one short).
4. **The detour** — "Our first customers were using GPUs back in 2017, 2018. They didn't call it
   AI yet." Autonomous driving, "these crazy GPU guys… as long as the money is good," then the
   GPT moment: "now we don't say it's a detour. This is like what we do." Founder story with a
   clean button.
5. **Off-taker** — Steven's question as the cold open: "You've observed there is a difference
   between a neocloud that serves a single off-taker as a tenant versus one that operates an
   independent cloud." Liran: they fund differently, underwrite on the off-taker, more capital
   efficient, "the end price is way lower… you're going to get a much better deal." Leading "So"
   trimmed by the in point.
6. **Shortage** — "At this point there is such a big shortage. Everyone's winning. But in the
   long term…" Supermarket brands analogy; ends "the neoclouds that have the right
   differentiator that customers want… are the ones that will end up winning."
