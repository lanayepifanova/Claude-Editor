1. **Anthropic valuation** — opens "If you have cheap flash models that are being widely
   deployed… that's going to comprise the vast majority of the tokens used." Through frontier
   labs' margins to "they're already being valued at somewhere between one to three trillion
   dollars," landing on "usage shifting away from frontier reasoning towards everyday agentic
   use." Hot-company hook with a clear thesis.
   *Optional cold open:* Phoebe at 7:36.9 "I saw a chart… token prices going down… affecting
   Anthropic's ARR. Can you break that down?" (≈9s).
2. **Power, not chips** — "If all of the compute rental rates start going down… maybe demand
   for AI isn't quite catching up with supply… literally the trillion-dollar question."
   Exponential demand vs. linear supply, Phoebe's "chips sitting there not plugged in?", and
   ends on her line "And Elon is running around the world looking for turbines."
3. **NYC apartment** — "If somebody had three GPUs and they rented out for $2.75 an hour, the
   next one I wouldn't just assume is $2.75." East Village vs. Soho vs. Harlem; ends "saving the
   landlord, the provider, the inconvenience of having to search for a new tenant all the time."
4. **Token index** — "It's our token index. It's actually kind of a misnomer… It's neither"
   (not price, not volume). Luxury car vs. ordinary sedan; ends "you're going to see a lot more
   cars on the street." Natural part 1 to #1 (they abut; no overlap).
5. **Glorified regression** — Phoebe: "How do you consolidate those into indices?… Not asking
   for your secret recipe." Steven: "We call it machine learning, but it is basically glorified
   regression." Then the top five features; ends "a number of key features that we deem as
   important." Host question as built-in cold open.
6. **Paying for data** — Phoebe: "Do you pay these providers for their data?" Small providers
   swap data for market intelligence, mid-size half in-kind, big ones commercial; ends "a bigger
   market fundamentally is much better for everybody."
