   and down 20%, 50%… uncertainty is what kills business planning." Ends "you can use futures
   to hedge that… it's actually really good for the whole ecosystem." Natural part 2 to #1;
   different point (hedging for buyers vs. Nvidia's ROI).
4. **Share prices** — Phoebe: "If I were a neocloud, I don't want to share pricing data with
   anyone besides the customers. How do you convince them?" Carmen: "We 100% anonymize it…
   even the neocloud provider does not know if their data points are used." Ends on the
   separate index committee.
5. **3 → 300** — "If you look at the public comments… what if your data source is only three
   hyperscalers?… That was the case two and a half years ago… now we have over 300 neocloud
   providers on our platform." Number payoff.
6. **Billion RFQ** — "All activity has been going up through the roof. We have seen over a
   billion notional of RFQ every single month… one node all the way to 20,000 nodes… activity-
   wise it's definitely going to be hotter." Prediction close.
